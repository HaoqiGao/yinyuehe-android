设备保存的 result marker 时序为：

| Marker | Connection | Generation | Disconnect edges | Emissions | Queue |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| `reconnect-generation-1.result` | `CONNECTED` | 1 | 0 | 1 | empty |

Proto 也没有被旧 generation 回写：

